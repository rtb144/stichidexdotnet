Stitchidex — Your Embroidery Pattern Companion

Getting Started Guide for Windows 10 & Windows 11

You don't need to be a "computer person" to use Stitchidex. This guide walks you through each step slowly. Take your time. Nothing here can harm your patterns: Stitchidex only reads your files and never changes or deletes them.

1. Welcome to Stitchidex

Stitchidex gathers all of your embroidery patterns into one cozy, visual catalog. You'll see a picture of each design with its real thread colors, so you can find the right pattern without opening each file.

It works with the files your machine uses, including:

.pes (Brother, Baby Lock), .jef (Janome), .vp3 and .hus (Husqvarna Viking, Pfaff), .exp (Bernina), .dst, .xxx, and more.

Everything happens on your own computer. You don't need internet or an account, and nothing is uploaded.

2. Installing Stitchidex

  1. Download StichidexDotNet-Setup-0.5.0-win64.exe (the download link is on the Stitchidex web page).
  2. Open your Downloads folder and double-click the file.
  3. Windows may show a blue box that says "Windows protected your PC." This is normal for new programs from small makers.
  • Click the small words More info.
  • Then click the Run anyway button.
  1. If Windows asks "Do you want to allow this app to make changes?", click Yes.
  2. Click Next and then Install. When it says it's finished, click Finish.
  3. From now on, open Stitchidex by double-clicking the Stitchidex (.NET) picture on your Desktop, or by finding it in the Start menu.

Tip: A printable copy of this guide is in the Start menu as Stitchidex User Guide.

Your whole pattern collection at a glance, with a large preview on the right

3. Adding Your Pattern Folders

  1. Click the + Add Folder button.
  2. A window opens. Find the folder where your patterns live, for example Documents → Embroidery or Downloads. Click it once, then click Select Folder.
  3. A progress bar appears at the top while Stitchidex looks through your patterns. With a large collection this can take a few minutes. You can keep using the program while it works.
  4. That's it! Stitchidex remembers your folders. When you open it next time, all your patterns will already be there.

You can add as many folders as you like, including a USB stick or an external drive.

4. Finding the Perfect Pattern (Smart Search & Filters)

Searching with everyday words

Click in the Search box at the top and type what you're looking for in plain words:

  • winter finds snowmen, snowflakes, reindeer, mittens, and sleighs
  • construction finds diggers, dump trucks, bulldozers, and cranes
  • floral finds roses, daisies, sunflowers, and bouquets
  • baby, halloween, kitchen, beach, monogram, and more

Smart Search understands the idea behind your words, not just the exact letters. It also uses your folder names, so a pattern inside a folder called "Christmas" will show up for christmas.

Made a typo? No problem

If you type wintar or snoman, Stitchidex still finds your patterns. You'll see "Did you mean: winter?" with round buttons under the search box. Click a button to use that word.

Smart Search: the typo "wintar" still finds your winter designs and offers "Did you mean: winter?"

Narrowing things down

On the left side you can choose:

  • Hoop size: 4x4, 5x7, 6x10, and so on, so you only see designs that fit your hoop
  • Format: only .pes files, only .jef files, etc.
  • Craft Tag: your own labels (see section 5)

Click Reset Filters to see everything again.

Light or dark screen

If the screen feels too dark, choose Light from the theme picker to get a bright, white background.

The bright Warm Atelier Light theme

5. Looking at a Pattern & Adding Notes and Tags

  • Click a pattern once to see a large stitch preview on the right, along with its size, stitch count, and thread colors.
  • Change the fabric background (Dark, White, Linen, or Black) to see how the design will look on your fabric.
  • Add a tag: type a word such as Christmas 2026, Made for Sarah, or Baby Blanket and click Add. Tags help you find patterns again later.
  • Tag several patterns at once: hold down the Ctrl key on your keyboard and click each pattern you want. Then click Tag Selected.
  • Notes: type anything you'd like to remember, such as "use metallic thread" or "stitched for the church bazaar."

Tagging three selected patterns at once

6. Sending a Pattern to Your USB Stick

  1. Plug your USB flash drive into the computer.
  2. Click the pattern (or Ctrl+Click several), then click Send to USB Drive.
  3. Choose your USB stick, then choose the folder your machine expects:
  • Root of drive (the main folder) for most machines
  • Embroidery for some Janome and Brother models
  • bPocket for Brother machines that use it
  1. Click Copy to USB. You can also print or save the Printable Thread Color Chart to keep next to your machine.
  2. Before you unplug the stick, click Safely Eject USB and wait for the message that says it's safe to remove.

Send to USB: choose your machine's folder and include a printable thread chart

7. Helpful Tips & Troubleshooting

My patterns don't appear.

Make sure you added the folder that actually holds your files (section 3). Click Rescan All to look again.

A pattern shows no picture.

Some very old or unusual files can't be previewed. You can still search for them and send them to USB.

Search finds nothing.

Try a simpler word (for example snow instead of snowy winter wonderland), and click Reset Filters in case a hoop or format filter is still switched on.

Windows blocked the installer.

Click More info and then Run anyway (see section 2).

Will it change or delete my files?

No. Stitchidex only reads your patterns. Sending to USB makes a copy.

Does it need the internet?

No. Everything, including Smart Search, works offline.

How do I remove it?

Open Start → Settings → Apps, find Stitchidex (.NET), and click Uninstall.
