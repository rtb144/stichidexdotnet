## 4. Finding the Perfect Pattern (Smart Search & Filters)

### Searching with everyday words
Click in the **Search** box at the top and type what you're looking for in plain words:

- **winter** finds snowmen, snowflakes, reindeer, mittens, and sleighs
- **construction** finds diggers, dump trucks, bulldozers, and cranes
- **floral** finds roses, daisies, sunflowers, and bouquets
- **baby**, **halloween**, **kitchen**, **beach**, **monogram**, and more

Smart Search understands the *idea* behind your words, not just the exact letters. It also uses your folder names, so a pattern inside a folder called "Christmas" will show up for **christmas**.

### Made a typo? No problem
If you type **wintar** or **snoman**, Stitchidex still finds your patterns. You'll see **"Did you mean: winter?"** with round buttons under the search box. Click a button to use that word.

### Narrowing things down
On the left side you can choose:
- **Hoop size:** 4x4, 5x7, 6x10, and so on, so you only see designs that fit your hoop
- **Format:** only .pes files, only .jef files, etc.
- **Craft Tag:** your own labels (see section 5)

Click **Reset Filters** to see everything again.

### Light or dark screen
If the screen feels too dark, choose **Light** from the theme picker to get a bright, white background.

## 5. Looking at a Pattern & Adding Notes and Tags

- **Click a pattern once** to see a large stitch preview on the right, along with its size, stitch count, and thread colors.
- Change the **fabric background** (Dark, White, Linen, or Black) to see how the design will look on your fabric.
- **Add a tag:** type a word such as *Christmas 2026*, *Made for Sarah*, or *Baby Blanket* and click **Add**. Tags help you find patterns again later.
- **Tag several patterns at once:** hold down the **Ctrl** key on your keyboard and click each pattern you want. Then click **Tag Selected**.
- **Notes:** type anything you'd like to remember, such as "use metallic thread" or "stitched for the church bazaar."
