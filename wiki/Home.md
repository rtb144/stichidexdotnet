## 1. Welcome to Stitchidex

Stitchidex gathers all of your embroidery patterns into one cozy, visual catalog. You'll see a picture of each design with its real thread colors, so you can find the right pattern without opening each file.

It works with the files your machine uses, including:
**.pes** (Brother, Baby Lock), **.jef** (Janome), **.vp3** and **.hus** (Husqvarna Viking, Pfaff), **.exp** (Bernina), **.dst**, **.xxx**, and more.

Everything happens on your own computer. You don't need internet or an account, and nothing is uploaded.
